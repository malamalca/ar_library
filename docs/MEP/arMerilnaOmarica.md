# arMerilnaOmarica

Meter cabinet (merilna omarica). Standard utility meter enclosure in two sizes: **PMO3** (0.45 × 0.18 m) and **PMO4** (0.6 × 0.18 m); selecting the kind sets A and B automatically.

**Source:** [`Library_Src/MEP/arMerilnaOmarica/`](../../Library_Src/MEP/arMerilnaOmarica/)

## Parameters

| Parameter | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| `kind` | PMO3 / PMO4 |
| `A`, `B` | Dimensions (set by kind) |
